# GMC Brand Pages Progress Report

## Completed

We reviewed the 20 supplied brand content files and connected them to the existing GMC brand-page design.

- 16 brand pages were successfully integrated and are working.
- The existing Mercedes-Benz and BMW pages were kept in place.
- The other available brand content was added without creating separate page code for each brand.

## Pages not added

Four brand files could not be published:

- Fiat
- Honda
- MG
- Mitsubishi

The supplied information for these brands did not include enough usable engine stock or sales information to create a helpful page. Empty or misleading pages were therefore not published.

## Homepage brand links

Most homepage brand links now open their dedicated brand pages. Jaguar, Range Rover, and Ford are still shown in the homepage brand list, but their dedicated pages are not available yet.

## “See All Brands” status

The project does not currently have a dedicated All Brands page. The existing button sends visitors back to the homepage brand section. This prevents a broken page but does not provide a complete brand directory, so it can appear as if the button does nothing.

## Recommendation

Create a dedicated All Brands page showing the 16 available brand pages in one list. At the same time, either add content for Jaguar, Range Rover, and Ford or remove/label those cards until their content is available. The four unavailable brands should remain hidden until suitable engine information is provided.
