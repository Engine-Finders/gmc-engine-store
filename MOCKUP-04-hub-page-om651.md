## OM651 Engine Buying FAQs

**Q: Which vehicles use the Mercedes OM651 engine?**  
A: The OM651 is a 2.1-litre four-cylinder diesel fitted across Mercedes-Benz's range between 2008 and 2018. It covers the C-Class (W204 and W205), E-Class (W212), GLK and early GLC, SLK R172, ML/GLE W166, the A-Class/CLA/GLA family in transverse form, and van specification in the Sprinter W906 and Vito/V-Class. Each application is sold as its own configured unit, so buy at the listed price for your exact vehicle.

**Q: Is the OM651 the same engine in a car and a van?**  
A: The core architecture is shared, but car, transverse and van versions are not interchangeable as complete units - sump design, turbo arrangement, ancillary brackets and injector specification differ between them. That's why this engine is sold as separate application-specific units rather than one generic listing, each configured and priced for its own vehicle.

**Q: Should I buy a used or reconditioned OM651?**  
A: Both are available depending on the application. A used engine is removed from a donor vehicle and tested before sale; a reconditioned engine is fully stripped, machined and rebuilt with new wear parts and carries a 12-month, unlimited-mileage warranty. Each application page shows which conditions are currently available and at what price, so you can buy at the listed price that suits your budget.

**Q: How do I confirm an OM651 fits my vehicle?**  
A: Enter your registration number in the checker on this page or on any application listing - it confirms your exact model, year and factory-fitted engine code from DVLA vehicle data before you order. Every application page also lists its full compatible-model range, so you can check directly before you buy.

**Q: What warranty and delivery come with an OM651 engine?**  
A: Reconditioned OM651 units carry a 12-month, unlimited-mileage warranty; used units state their own warranty term on the listing before you buy. Delivery is UK mainland by kerbside pallet, typically 2 to 4 working days for in-stock units, and any engine that proves incompatible can be returned uninstalled within 14 days for a full refund.
